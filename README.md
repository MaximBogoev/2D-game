# Monke banna Rise 35 BYA
35 million years ago monkeys rose to power, but why? they must have had motivation and therefore a motive. this game shall show that.

## Game Idea 
Monke chill with monke friends, all have bananas, digg. Then come lion, he ruuinin it. Me monke angwy!
Me want to take over, challenge and beat lion, 1 problem tho. Lion is the king of the jungle. uh oh...
But what monke have that he dont, is brain. think Mark, THINK! 
Now we look at the list of who has most rep, (NFS: Most Wanted inspired) 
and we have to defeat every boss on the list 
working our way up before we can take on the lion, the king at the top of the list.

## Fighting mechanics Ideas
sloss med dem faktisk på kartet noen slasher med sine naturlige appearances, 
mens andre (gorilla f.eks) kaster ting på deg (som Zeke fra Attack On Titan)

### Weapon Ideas
* fangs - spørs på dyret hvor mye damage det gjør
* hale - spørs på dyret hvor mye damage det gjør
* fists - kangaroo boxer
* tenner - spørs på dyret hvor mye damage det gjør
* banana - vanlig banan som gjør litt damage
* banna - bananen snudd opp ned med den tuppen pekene ut å stabbe med (mer damage en banan)
